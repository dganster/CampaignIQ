CampaignIQ Constitution
Preamble

CampaignIQ is founded on the belief that investing is fundamentally a series of decisions made under conditions of uncertainty.

Every investment campaign reflects countless decisions: when to commit capital, how to manage risk, when to adjust, and when to exit.

Those decisions create a history.

CampaignIQ exists to help investors understand that history.

This Constitution defines the enduring principles that guide the design and evolution of CampaignIQ. These principles should remain stable even as technologies, markets, and investment products change.

Whenever an important design decision is made, the first question should be:

Is this consistent with the Constitution of CampaignIQ?

Mission

CampaignIQ is a Decision Analysis platform for investors.

Traditional investment software helps investors execute trades or decide what to do next.

CampaignIQ addresses a different problem.

It reconstructs investment campaigns, analyzes the decisions that shaped them, measures the effectiveness of capital deployment, and reveals patterns that help investors continuously improve their judgment.

CampaignIQ does not tell investors how to invest.

It helps them understand how they invest.

Vision

CampaignIQ exists to improve investor judgment.

It accomplishes this not by replacing judgment with automation, but by helping investors better understand the consequences of their own decisions.

Every campaign becomes an opportunity to learn.

Every decision becomes evidence.

Every outcome becomes feedback.

Learning is the product.

Guiding Principles
1. Respect the Language of Investing

CampaignIQ adopts established industry terminology whenever practical.

Terms such as order, execution, trade, position, assignment, expiration, roll, and campaign should be used consistently with their accepted meaning.

Innovation comes from deeper analysis—not from inventing new vocabulary.

2. Preserve Facts. Derive Knowledge.

Facts are permanent.

Knowledge evolves.

CampaignIQ preserves objective facts and derives interpretations from those facts.

As analytical methods improve, new knowledge can always be derived from the same historical record.

Lost facts can never be recovered.

3. Remain Methodology Neutral

CampaignIQ does not advocate any investment philosophy.

It supports value investors, growth investors, options traders, futures traders, dividend investors, and methodologies yet to be imagined.

CampaignIQ evaluates decisions without judging strategies.

4. Capital Is the Investor's Scarce Resource

Every investment campaign represents a commitment of capital.

CampaignIQ seeks to understand not only whether capital produced a profit, but how effectively it was employed over time.

Profit is important.

Capital efficiency is equally important.

5. Analysis Before Advice

CampaignIQ performs Decision Analysis.

It does not provide investment advice.

Its responsibility is to transform investing history into understanding.

The investor remains responsible for every decision.

The Learning Cycle

CampaignIQ is founded upon a simple belief:

Better decisions come from better understanding.

Understanding comes from disciplined analysis.

Analysis transforms experience into knowledge.

Knowledge strengthens judgment.

Improved judgment leads to better future decisions.

Our Aspiration

Every investor possesses a lifetime of valuable experience.

Too often that experience remains trapped in brokerage statements, spreadsheets, and fading memories.

CampaignIQ exists to unlock that experience.

By faithfully reconstructing investment campaigns and applying disciplined Decision Analysis, CampaignIQ transforms experience into understanding, understanding into judgment, and judgment into wiser investing.

Experience is not the best teacher.

Understood experience is.

CampaignIQ will never replace the investor's judgment. It exists to strengthen it.
